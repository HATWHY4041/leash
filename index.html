Type “pay Tunde 20 for the logo”. Leash's assistant pays approved people instantly, inside limits you set on-chain. Anything bigger waits for your approval. One tap freezes it.

  • Allowlist — it can only pay people you approved
  • Daily limit — a hard cap per 24 hours
  • Approval threshold — big payments need your signature
  • Kill switch — pause or revoke the AI key anytime

Use MetaMask (desktop or the MetaMask app's browser). Leash adds the Aevum network for you.
