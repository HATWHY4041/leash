Four rules the AI can't talk its way around

Approved payees
It can only pay addresses you added. A tricked prompt can't send money to a stranger.
Daily limit
A hard cap for every 24 hours, however many times it's asked.
Ask-me amount
Bigger payments become requests that you approve or reject with your own wallet.
Pause and revoke
Freeze the assistant or replace its key in one tap. The old key stops working.

These live in your own contract, not in the AI. Even if the assistant's key leaked, the most anyone could send is today's limit, and only to people you trust.
